How I Work

How I Work

From ambiguity to shipped product

My process is research-first, then structure, then build and measure.

1

Talk to users. Interviews, surveys, watching people actually try to do the thing.

2

Map the problem. Competitive analysis, workflow mapping, identifying where friction lives.

3

Scope and prioritize. RICE scoring, PRDs with data-backed specs, cutting scope until the MVP is sharp.

4

Build and test. Sprint planning, Figma prototypes, iterative testing with real users.

5

Measure and iterate. KPI dashboards, usage analytics, then back to step 1.

Tools that support this: Figma, Notion, Python, SQL, QuickSight, A/B testing frameworks
